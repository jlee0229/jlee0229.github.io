Humanpath

Walking routes in Boston and Austin, scored block by block.

Summer 2026 · Try it · Code

You enter a start and an end, or use your current position, and Humanpath finds several walking routes. It scores each one block by block for surface type, safety, and overall walkability, then recommends one. I made it for phones, and it covers Boston and Austin.

Scoring a route

A route's score isn't the average of its blocks. One bad stretch pulls the whole route down, so a few good blocks can't hide it. A slider lets you trade distance for a better walk.
